# Product

## Register

brand

> Mixed surface: `/` is brand (the thesis sells the work), `/explore` and `/math` are
> product (a tool + a paper). Design IS part of the product — this is a portfolio piece
> judged on taste — so the default register is `brand`, applied with product discipline.

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers (grasp depth + taste in ~20s), quant reviewers (trace every
number to its math, citation, and OOS-honest backtest), and DIY investors (see a real
diversified portfolio built and explained, no signup). They arrive skeptical of finance
demos; the interface has to earn trust through precision, not polish-for-its-own-sake.

## Product Purpose
Argmin builds portfolios with seven from-scratch optimizers and proves them with honest,
cost-aware, walk-forward out-of-sample backtests, then shows the math. Success = a reviewer
believes the numbers and the engineering, and a recruiter remembers the taste.

## Brand Personality
Precise, declarative, honest. Reads like a quant research note typeset in a journal, not a
SaaS landing. Voice states what the engine does and what the numbers mean; surfaces
limitations in the open (OOS-only, costs applied, "not investment advice"). No hype verbs.

## Anti-references
- **The Vercel/Next.js default look** (Geist Sans, soft ghost-cards, tinted pill badges,
  an uppercase tracked eyebrow over every section). This is the exact "AI slop" the
  redesign exists to remove.
- Generic fintech navy-and-gold dashboards; gradient hero metrics; identical card grids.
- Anything that reads "AI made that" at a glance.

## Design Principles
1. **Typeset, don't decorate.** The page should feel like a paper that happens to be
   interactive. Equations are first-class; prose harmonizes with them.
2. **Honesty is a surface, not a footnote.** Provenance (window, costs, rf, engine hash,
   OOS) sits next to the numbers.
3. **One signal, used sparingly.** Amber marks the chosen strategy / tangency / primary
   action only — as ink and markers, never as decorative fills.
4. **Hairlines over cards.** Separate regions with rules and rhythm; reach for a boxed
   panel only when the data truly needs a container. Never nest them.
5. **Every number binds to live engine output.** No invented precision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, on the locked dark theme. Visible accent focus
rings. Full keyboard operation of all controls. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every
animation. Chart series distinguishable beyond color (markers, labels, position).
